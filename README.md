## Repository for data sets and solutions to programming problems for Astronomy 420: Introduction to Planetary and Exoplanetary Science. Taught in Fall 26 at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University in Prescott, AZ.

### Some these were taken either from Salyk & Lewis' _Introductory Notes on Planetary Science_ or from Lissauer & de Pater's _Fundamental Planetary Science_ (updated edition). For clarity, here are the problems that correspond to some of these notebooks:

* ### `Crater Counting.ipynb` Salyk & Lewis Chatper 7, Problem 1.
* ### `Jean's Escape.ipynb` Lissauer & de Pater Chapter 5, Problem 15
* ### `Minimum Mass Solar Nebula.ipynb` Salyk & Lewis Chatper 9, Problem 3.

### Additional problems and solutions can be found on my website.
